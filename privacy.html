What we collect

When you send an inquiry, request a purchase, apply for financing or ask for an offer, we collect the details you type: your name, phone, email and address, and for financing, employment, income and your Social Security number.

We also use a few cookies to remember your saved cars and to understand which pages are useful.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your questions, prepare paperwork, arrange delivery and, if you ask us to, find financing. We don't sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the partners needed to complete what you asked for: up to three lending partners you approve, licensed transport carriers, and the state agencies that handle titles and registration.

How long we keep it

Identity documents are deleted after the sale, or within 30 days if you don't buy. Your Social Security number is not kept after a financing decision. Sales records are kept as long as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time, and you can stop texts by replying STOP.
