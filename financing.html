Financing questions

Will applying hurt my credit score?
Prequalifying uses a soft check where our lenders support it, which doesn't affect your score. A full application with your chosen lender may involve a hard inquiry, and we tell you before that happens.
Can I get financing with bad credit or no credit?
Often, yes. Some of our partners specialize in first-time buyers and credit rebuilding. A larger down payment or a co-signer can help.
Can I use my own bank or credit union?
Absolutely. Bring a pre-approval letter and we'll work with your lender directly.
Is there a penalty for paying off early?
None of our current lending partners charge a prepayment penalty, but always confirm in your loan contract.
