Delivery questions

How long does delivery take?
Most deliveries arrive within 2 to 9 days depending on distance. We confirm a window before the car leaves and text you along the way.
Is my car insured during transport?
Yes. Every carrier we use is licensed and carries cargo insurance. The car stays our responsibility until you sign for it.
What should I check when it arrives?
Walk around the car with the driver and compare it to the photos we send before pickup. Note anything new on the delivery receipt before signing, then call us.
