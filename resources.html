Buying guides

Frequently asked questions

Buying

Is the listed price the price I pay?
Yes, plus only state taxes, title and registration. Our documentation fee is already included, and we never add surprise add-ons.
Can I negotiate?
We price every car close to market from the start, so most buyers find there's little to negotiate. If you've seen a comparable car for less, tell us and we'll take a look.
Can I have my own mechanic inspect the car?
Absolutely. We're happy to drive it to a shop near us or let your mechanic come to the lot.
How do I book a test drive?
Call or text us, or choose "Test drive or visit" on the contact form. We'll have the car charged or fueled and ready when you arrive.

Financing

Will checking my rate affect my credit score?
Prequalifying uses a soft check where available, which doesn't affect your score. We tell you before any hard inquiry happens.
What do I need to apply?
Your address, employment and income details, and your Social Security number at the final step. Lenders may later ask for a recent pay stub.

Trade-ins

Can I trade in a car I still owe money on?
Yes. We pay your lender directly and apply any remaining value to your next car.
How long is my offer valid?
Seven days or 250 more miles, whichever comes first, as long as the car matches what you described.

Delivery

Do you deliver outside Nebraska?
Yes, to all 50 states with insured carriers. We also handle out-of-state title and registration paperwork.
What if the car arrives damaged?
Note it on the delivery receipt and call us. The car is our responsibility until you sign, and the carrier's insurance covers transport damage.

Warranty

Do your cars come with a warranty?
Every car includes a 90-day or 3,000-mile limited powertrain warranty (example terms). Many also have remaining factory coverage, which we list on each car.
How does the 7-day exchange work?
If the car isn't right, bring it back within 7 days and 300 miles and swap it for another one from our inventory (example terms).
