What affects your offer

A few plain things move the number most. Being upfront helps us get it right the first time.

Mileage and age

Lower miles for the year usually means a higher offer.

Accident history

We check the history report, so mention any repairs.

Service records

Receipts for regular maintenance add real value.

Tires and warning lights

Worn tires or a check-engine light lower the offer.

Common questions

What paperwork do I need?
Your title (or loan details if a lender holds it), a valid photo ID, current registration and all keys. We handle the rest of the forms.
What if I still owe money on my loan?
That's common. We pay your lender directly. If the offer is more than you owe, you get the difference. If it's less, you can pay the gap or roll it into your next car.
How fast do I get paid?
Once we confirm the car in person, we send a bank transfer, usually within one business day. A same-day check is available if you prefer.
Do I have to buy a car from you?
No. We buy cars outright every day, whether or not you're shopping with us.
